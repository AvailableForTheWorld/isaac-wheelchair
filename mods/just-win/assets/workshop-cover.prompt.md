## 完整提示词

```text
Create an original square Steam Workshop cover for an Isaac-inspired dungeon game mod. Make it look like a deliberately simple cover drawn by an indie modder with a black felt pen and a few flat fill colors. It must work at 128px thumbnail size. Use three readable elements only, with generous outside margins and a muted warm charcoal-brown background, small warm cream accents, no detailed scenery.
Top half and center: a giant thumbs-up next to an original dark-comedy dungeon cartoon kid, clearly and unmistakably giving the universal thumbs-up gesture toward the viewer with one hand. The kid is bald with a big rounded head, a little tear under one eye, beige/pale peach coloring, a crooked satisfied grin, and a plain small dark shirt. A rough, comic doodle with bold black outlines and slightly asymmetric forms, like a small game-zine mascot. Draw this as new illustration and a distinct silhouette, with no replication of any existing game sprite or official character artwork.
Bottom middle: exactly two compact isolated game item-quality badge shapes, side-by-side and visually equal. Make them like deliberately drawn quality rank icons, bold-black outlined beveled square/crest silhouettes with one subtle inset fill shade, no pedestals or items. LEFT is an unmistakably PURPLE badge with a large high-contrast cream number "3" centered. RIGHT is an unmistakably GOLDEN YELLOW badge with a large high-contrast dark-brown number "4" centered. Make both badges big enough to scan at a glance, no other ranks or symbols.
At the bottom, big cream handwritten bold lettering exactly "just win" (all lowercase, one line, no quote marks). Align the core group neatly and leave clean space around the edges. No extra captions, fake text, title or logo, border, glow, rim light, gradients, elaborate textures, particles, symmetry for its own sake, or photorealistic rendering. Value clear design, dry playful expression, and the look of a real person who drew it quickly and knew where to stop.
```
