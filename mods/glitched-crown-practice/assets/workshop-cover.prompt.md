# 错误王冠无尽模式封面

- 生成方式：内置 `image_gen` 工具。
- 输出：`workshop-cover.png`。
- 图标参考：[Glitched Crown — IsaacGuru](https://isaacguru.com/wiki/isaac/c689)，本地参考文件为 `glitched-crown-reference.png`。
- 设计意图：原版错误王冠像素图标加绿色正确勾，尽量简洁、接近玩家手工制作的 mod 封面。

## 完整提示词

```text
Use case: compositing / game mod cover.
Create a square 1024x1024 Steam Workshop cover by composing the provided ORIGINAL Glitched Crown collectible sprite from The Binding of Isaac with a clear correct-answer check mark.
Input image: the supplied 32px native game sprite is the crown identity reference and source icon to preserve. Enlarge its existing pixel-art design faithfully: preserve its distinctive asymmetrical, slightly broken/glitched GOLD/YELLOW crown silhouette and black/brown stepped outline. It is a tiny gold crown, NOT a silver jewel crown and NOT a generic symmetric royal crown. Do not reinterpret it as a detailed illustration. Preserve the chunky individual pixel blocks and limited original palette.
Composition: one large crown centered in the square, occupying roughly 55-60% of the cover width, with one bold GREEN check mark partially overlapping the lower right of the crown. The crown remains immediately recognizable, and the check mark clearly communicates selecting the correct item. The check mark should be a simple hand-placed pixel-art tick, matching the crown's coarse pixel grid, with a dark outline and at most two green shades. Good generous margins, legible at a tiny thumbnail size.
Background: completely flat muted dark warm charcoal-brown, close to Isaac's dungeon palette. No environment and no elaborate framing.
Art direction: looks like a clean manually assembled Aseprite mod icon. Deliberately simple, restrained, functional retro sprite composition, pixel-perfect hard edges and a small palette. Enlarge pixel clusters as nearest-neighbor blocks, no blurry edges or anti-aliasing. NO words, letters, title, captions, logos, watermark, sparkles, particles, radial burst, gradients, lens effects, glow, metallic rendering, painted texture, 3D extrusion, dramatic lighting, extra icons, or generative decorative details. ONLY the faithful gold Glitched Crown sprite plus the green tick on a flat background.
```
